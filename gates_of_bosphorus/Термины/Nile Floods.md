# Nile Floods

The **Flooding of the Nile** is a natural flooding cycle observed along the Lower Nile River resulting from the monsoon rains. Every few years, the Nile will experience the Наводнение [[vanilla/Термины/Условия сбора урожая|Условия сбора урожая]]. This can be counteracted by constructing Nile Barrage gbbf_buildings.

These floods will inflict the Widespread Nile Flooding [[vanilla/Термины/Модификатор|Модификатор]] according to the following flood stages:
- Negligible (1-2)
- Mild (3-4)
- Heavy (5-6)
- Severe (7-8)
- Catastrophic (9-10)
