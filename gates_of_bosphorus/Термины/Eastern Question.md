# Eastern Question

The Eastern Question is the unstable political situation caused by the decline of the Ottoman Empire. As the empire slowly crumbles from within, the gbbf_great_powers struggle to find strategic and political solutions to expand their influence without disrupting the [[gates_of_bosphorus/Термины/Concert of Europe|Concert of Europe]].
