# Beylik

**Beylik** is the name ascribed to a principality or petty kingdom ruled by a Bey. With the decline of the Byzantine Empire and Sultan of Rûm, beyliks came to dominate Anatolia before their annexation to the Ottoman Empire. Despite this, the title of *Bey* endures, itself often used to assert independence from Constantinople.
