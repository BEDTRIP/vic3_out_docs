# Bedouin Tribes

The **Бедуины Tribes** live in nomadic bands scattered across the Феццан and Египетская пустыня. Generally aloof to the maneuvering of the gbbf_great_powers, they tend to react poorly, even violently, once direct authority is imposed upon them.
