# Eyalet

The **Eyalets**, also called *Beylerbeyliks* or *Pashaliks*, are the primary administrative divisions of the Ottoman Empire. Originating in the years shortly after the Fall of Constantinople, the Eyalets function as a mixture of taxation and military administrations, with each headed by a Bey. While useful for the Empire's decentralized governance, this same system frequently hampers central authority.
