# Concert of Europe

The Concert of Europe is the informal political structure and balance of power that gives each country a [[vanilla/Термины/Ранг|Ранг]], provides each [[vanilla/Термины/Великая держава|Великая держава]] with a sphere of influence, and has been a major step towards avoiding the conditions that led to the Французск Revolutionary War and the Napoleonic Wars.
