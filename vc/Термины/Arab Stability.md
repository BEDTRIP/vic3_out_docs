# Arab Stability

[[vc/Термины/Arab Stability|Arab Stability]] represents the degree of control exercised by Османская Империя over the Arabian Peninsula and the obedience of the local population. The lower [[vc/Термины/Arab Stability|Arab Stability]] becomes, the more likely negative Arab events are to occur, and the easier it becomes for foreign powers to intervene in Arab affairs. When [[vc/Термины/Arab National Fervour|Arab National Fervour]] equals 0, [[vc/Термины/Arab Stability|Arab Stability]] will increase by 1% each month. [[vc/Термины/Arab Stability|Arab Stability]] can be tracked in the **the_unruly_arabs** [[vanilla/Термины/Запись в дневнике|Запись в дневнике]].
