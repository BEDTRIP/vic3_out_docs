# Caretaker Government

A caretaker government is a temporary [[vanilla/Термины/Правительство|Правительство]] formed during an [[vanilla/Термины/Выборы|Выборы]] period to maintain operations until a new administration is established. Lacking the legitimate [[vanilla/Термины/Выборы|Выборы]] mandate to exercise legislative authority, caretaker governments usually have limited powers, tasked with maintaining the status quo rather than truly governing the [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страна]] or enacting new [[vanilla/Термины/Закон|Закон]].
