# Yellow River Flood Zone

The Yellow River Flood Zone refers to the regions affected after the river’s course change, usually the directly impacted provinces of Henan, Shandong, and Northern Anhui, along with Jiangsu, indirectly affected by high water levels. All provinces in the flood zone except Jiangsu will suffer severe negative effects during floods, but may also gain [[vanilla/Термины/Пахотные земли|Пахотные земли]] as Yellow River control progresses.
