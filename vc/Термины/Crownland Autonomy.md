# Crownland Autonomy

A semi-sovereign territory bound solely through personal union with the [[vanilla/Термины/Правитель|Правитель]]. These entities maintain:& Local legislatures for domestic affairs& Royal veto power over legislation& Shared monarchical institutions.
While required to:& Surrender **50%** of [[vanilla/Термины/Казна|Казна]] revenue& Follow imperial foreign policy& Provide military levies; This arrangement dissolves automatically if the suzerain abandons Монархия governance.
