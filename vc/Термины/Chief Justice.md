# Chief Justice

The Chief Justice is a [[vanilla/Термины/Персонаж|Персонаж]] who presides over judicial affairs in the federal Supreme Court. Appointed by the [[vanilla/Термины/Правитель|Правитель]], the Chief Justice serves for life and cannot be dismissed or replaced unless they die or are exiled.

Under the Constitution, upon appointment, their other positions are automatically relinquished, and they serve the country solely as Chief Justice.

The Chief Justice gains influence over [[vanilla/Термины/Принятие законов|Принятие законов]] based on their personal [[vanilla/Термины/Идеология|Идеология]].
