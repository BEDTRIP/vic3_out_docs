# Garrisons State

A [[vc/Термины/Garrisons State|Garrisons State]] is an extension of the power of the French Empire in Europe. The leader of the Bonaparte family acts as its protector and assumes military responsibilities within its governed regions. These entities maintain:& Control over domestic policy& Limited treaty-making capacity& Obligatory military cooperation
While retaining:& Follow French foreign policy& **30%** [[vanilla/Термины/Казна|Казна]] contributions& Provide military levies; This relationship is automatically dissolved if the [[vanilla/Термины/Сюзерен|Сюзерен]] abolishes the Монархия.
