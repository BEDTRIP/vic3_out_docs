# Superpower

A [[tr/Термины/Superpower|Superpower]] is a nation that possesses unparalleled influence and capabilities on the global stage. Characterized by its dominant military strength, economic prowess, and political clout, a [[tr/Термины/Superpower|Superpower]] can shape international affairs and dictate the terms of global engagement. These nations often lead in technological innovation, military strength, cultural influence, and diplomatic initiatives, positioning themselves as key players in maintaining global stability and order. The status of a [[tr/Термины/Superpower|Superpower]] is not static; it requires continuous investment in defense, economy, and international relations to sustain its preeminence.
