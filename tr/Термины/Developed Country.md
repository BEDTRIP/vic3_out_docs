# Developed Country

A country is considered [[tr/Термины/Developed Country|Developed Country]] when it has reached a certain level of economic and social development. This includes factors such as GDP per capita, life expectancy, education levels, and industrialization. [[tr/Термины/Developed Country|Developed Country]] countries are typically characterized by advanced infrastructure, a diversified economy and a high standard of living and welfare for its citizens.
