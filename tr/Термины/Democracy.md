# Democracy

[[tr/Термины/Democracy|Democracy]] is a form of government in which power is vested in the people, either directly or through elected representatives. It emphasizes the principles of political equality, individual freedoms, and the rule of law. In a [[tr/Термины/Democracy|Democracy]], citizens have the right to participate in decision-making processes, express their opinions, and hold their leaders accountable. Democracies often feature regular elections, free speech, and protection of minority rights, fostering a society where diverse voices can be heard and respected.
