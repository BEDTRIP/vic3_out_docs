# Decolonization

[[tr/Термины/Decolonization|Decolonization]] is a process that refers to the political and social movements aimed at ending [[vanilla/Термины/Колония|Колониальная]] rule and establishing independence for colonized territories. It involves the dismantling of [[vanilla/Термины/Колония|Колониальная]] structures, the assertion of self-determination, and the restoration of local governance. The process of [[tr/Термины/Decolonization|Decolonization]] can be complex and multifaceted, often involving negotiations, conflicts, and the redefinition of national identities.
