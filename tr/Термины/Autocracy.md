# Autocracy

[[tr/Термины/Autocracy|Autocracy]] is a form of government in which absolute power is concentrated in the hands of a single ruler or a small group. In an [[tr/Термины/Autocracy|Autocracy]], the ruling authority has unchecked control over political, social, and economic aspects of society, often without the consent or participation of the governed. [[tr/Термины/Autocracy|Autocracy]] regimes may suppress dissent, limit freedoms, and centralize decision-making, leading to a lack of accountability and potential abuses of power. This form of governance contrasts sharply with [[tr/Термины/Democracy|Democracy]] systems, where power is distributed among the people and their elected representatives.
