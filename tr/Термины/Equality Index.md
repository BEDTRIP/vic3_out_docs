# Equality Index

[[tr/Термины/Equality Index|Equality Index]] is a measure of the level of social and economic equality within [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страна]]. A higher [[tr/Термины/Equality Index|Equality Index]] indicates a more equitable distribution of wealth, opportunities, and resources among the population, while a lower index suggests significant disparities and social stratification. The [[tr/Термины/Equality Index|Equality Index]] is influenced by various factors, including government policies, social programs, and cultural attitudes towards equality and justice.
