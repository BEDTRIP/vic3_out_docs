# Liberty Index

[[tr/Термины/Liberty Index|Liberty Index]] represents the level of personal and political freedoms enjoyed by the citizens of [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страна]]. An higher [[tr/Термины/Liberty Index|Liberty Index]] indicates a more open and [[tr/Термины/Democracy|Democracy]] society, while a lower index suggests a more [[tr/Термины/Autocracy|Autocracy]] regime, a dictatorship, or even a dystopia. The [[tr/Термины/Liberty Index|Liberty Index]] is influenced by most of the laws in place within the country.
