# Environment

[[tr/Термины/Environment|Environment]] is a concept that refers to the natural world and the impact of human activity on it. It encompasses the air, water, land, and ecosystems that support life on [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страна]] and Earth in general. The environment is crucial for the survival of all living beings, and its health is essential for the well-being of future generations. Environmentalist movements advocate for its protection, preservation and restoration, often in opposition to industrialization and economic development that can harm natural resources.
