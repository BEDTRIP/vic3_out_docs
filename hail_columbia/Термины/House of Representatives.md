# House of Representatives

The House of Representatives is the lower house of the [[hail_columbia/Термины/Congress|Congress]] of the United States. During the Victorian era, the number of voting representatives in the House expanded over time to proportionally represent the population of the growing country.

The House's exclusive powers include initiating all revenue bills, impeaching federal officers, and electing the president if no candidate receives a majority of votes in the Electoral College.
