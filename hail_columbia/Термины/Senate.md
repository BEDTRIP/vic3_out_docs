# Senate

The Senate is the upper house of the [[hail_columbia/Термины/Congress|Congress]] of the United States. Seats in the senate are fixed at 2 per state in the union, acting to amplify the power of smaller states in constrast to the population-based [[hail_columbia/Термины/House of Representatives|House of Representatives]]. As Slavery became a definition issue in the nation, the allocation of new senate seats between Free and Slave states became a key issue, as neither side were keen to see the other gain a majority.

The Senate has exclusive power to confirm U.S. presidential appointments to high offices, approve or reject treaties, and try cases of impeachment brought by the House.
