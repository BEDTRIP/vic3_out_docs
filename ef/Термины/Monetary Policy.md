# Monetary Policy

The Monetary Policy is the primary mean that allows you to manage your inflation/deflation. It also enables you to increase or decrease your [[ef/Термины/Money Supply|Money Supply]], which will influence the [[ef/Термины/Money Value|Money Value]] your currency. It is the principal mean of achieving your [[ef/Термины/Metal Coverage Ratio|Metal Coverage Ratio]].
