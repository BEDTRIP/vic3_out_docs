# Financial Centre

In the 19th century, a [[ef/Термины/Financial Centre|Financial Centre]] was an economically significant region, where banking, financial and commercial activities were concentrated. These [[ef/Термины/Financial Centre|Financial Centre]] were often hubs for banking institutions, financial markets, corporations, and other business players.
