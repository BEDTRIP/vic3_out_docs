# Central Bank

In the 19th century, a [[ef/Термины/Central Bank|Central Bank]] was generally defined as a economic institution with the primary role of issuing the national currency, regulating the [[ef/Термины/Money Supply|Money Supply]], overseeing the financial system, and maintaining economic stability. [[ef/Термины/Central Bank|Central Bank]] of the 19th century were often established in response to the need for a central issuer of currency capable of controlling the [[ef/Термины/Money Supply|Money Supply]] and stabilizing the economic system.
