# BCBR

[[ef/Термины/Base Rate Percentage|Base Rate Percentage]] is percentage of the central bank (Bank rate, discount rate or base rate) which makes it possible to reduce the growth of the money supply by reducing credits granted to private banks. Its increase will slow down the economy and the monetary mass produced. Conversely, its reduction will strongly boost the economy and allow a private capitalist sector to appear.
