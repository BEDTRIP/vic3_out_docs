# Exchange Rate

In the 19th century, exchange rates between currencies were generally determined by a fixed exchange rate system based on the gold standard. This meant that the value of national currencies was fixed in relation to a specific quantity of gold. Governments often priced their national currencies in gold, thereby ensuring its convertibility.
