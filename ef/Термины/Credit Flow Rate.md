# Credit Flow Rate

The [[ef/Термины/Credit Flow Rate|Credit Flow Rate]] measures the net change in credit within the investment pool.

 - A positive rate indicates credit expansion, expressed as a percentage of the money supply.
 - A negative rate indicates credit contraction, expressed as a percentage of the investment pool.

 The rate is neutral when the base rate is between 6% and 7%.
 Below 6%, it turns positive and stimulates credit expansion; above 7%, it turns negative and leads to a contraction of the pool.
