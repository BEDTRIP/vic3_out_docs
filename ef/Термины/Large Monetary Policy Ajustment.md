# Large Monetary Policy Ajustment

A significant change implemented by a central bank in its monetary policy tools, such as substantial adjustments to interest rates, major alterations in reserve requirements, or large-scale open market operations. These adjustments are typically made in response to serious economic conditions or crises, aiming to bring about significant changes in economic activity.
