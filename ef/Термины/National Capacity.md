# National Capacity

The National Capacity represents the total amount of gold that the country possesses. It is determined by several factors: the [[ef/Термины/Government Treasury|Government Treasury]], the [[ef/Термины/Central Bank Metal Reserves|Central Bank Metal Reserves]], and the [[ef/Термины/Trade Balance|Trade Balance]].
