# Inflation Genrated

If the reserves of the **`GetPlayer.GetCustom('currency_name')` `GetPlayer.GetCustom('currency_symbol')`** held by the Central Bank fall below **10%**, the effects of the devaluation or revaluation policy no longer impact [[ef/Термины/Inflation Value|Inflation Value]]. This is because the reserves managed by the central bank are too small relative to the total money supply to meaningfully influence inflation
