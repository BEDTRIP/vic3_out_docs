# Country Rating

The Country Rating is a score assigned to a country based on its GDP, the value of its currency, inflation, prestige, rank, and standard of living. Based on the assigned rating, it will determine a threshold interest rate for the country. The interest rate that the country can achieve cannot be lower than the interest rate determined by the rating.
