# Maximum number of companies

The [[ef/Термины/Maximum number of companies|Maximum number of companies]]: represents the upper limit of businesses that the national financial system can support. This limit is determined by the sum of all financial center level, adjusted by the factor (1 – base rate).

Lower base rates allow a larger number of enterprises to operate, while higher base rates restrict this capacity.

If the number of existing enterprises exceeds this threshold, companies above the limit will randomly collapse due to insufficient credit support.
