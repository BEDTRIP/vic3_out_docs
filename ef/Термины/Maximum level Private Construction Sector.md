# Maximum level Private Construction Sector

The maximum level of Private Construction Sector is determined by the number of Городские центры present in the state and by the [[ef/Термины/Base Rate Percentage|Base Rate Percentage]]. For a [[ef/Термины/Base Rate Percentage|Base Rate Percentage]] of 1% or less than 1%, the maximum level is the number of Городские центры. For a [[ef/Термины/Base Rate Percentage|Base Rate Percentage]] of 10%, the maximum level of Private Construction Sector is 1.
