# Government Treasury

A [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страны]] weekly budget surplus is accumulated in its [[ef/Термины/Government Treasury|Government Treasury]]. If the budget balance becomes negative, any [[vanilla/Термины/Деньги|Деньги]] in the [[ef/Термины/Government Treasury|Government Treasury]] will be depleted first before the [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страна]] starts taking [[vanilla/Термины/Займы|Займы]].
