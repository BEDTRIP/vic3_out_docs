# Small Monetary Policy Ajustment

A minor change implemented by a central bank in its monetary policy tools, such as interest rates, reserve requirements, or open market operations, aimed at fine-tuning economic conditions. These adjustments are typically made to address short-term economic fluctuations without significantly altering the overall monetary policy stance.
