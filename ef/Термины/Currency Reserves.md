# Currency Reserves

The Currency Reserves is the total amount of foreign currency you possess, whether acquired through purchase or obtained through trade. You accumulate foreign currency only if a foreign country wishes to import goods from your market. In this case, the foreign country buys your goods, paying with its own currency. The same principle applies in the opposite direction if you import goods from another market; you transfer your currency to that country.
