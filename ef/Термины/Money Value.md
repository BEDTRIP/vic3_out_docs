# Money Value

The [[ef/Термины/Money Value|Money Value]], is determined by the [[ef/Термины/National Stockpile|National Stockpile]] divided by the total amount of currency produced and in circulation in your market ([[ef/Термины/Money Supply|Money Supply]]). If national currency exit of your market, the value of the currency will increase; if national currency enter your market, the value of the currency will decrease. In the same way, if you increase the production of currency, it will decrease the monetary value, while decreasing the production will increase the monetary value.
