# Maximum Private Construction Sector Capacity

The [[ef/Термины/Maximum Private Construction Sector Capacity|Maximum Private Construction Sector Capacity]]: represents the theoretical maximum level of Private Construction Sector that the national economy can sustain. This limit is determined by the total sum of Urban Center levels within the country, adjusted by the factor (1 – base rate).

 Lower base rates increase the construction system’s efficiency and allow more Private Construction Sector to operate, while higher base rates reduce this capacity.

 If the active number of PCS exceeds this threshold, excess sectors will stall or collapse due to insufficient credit and construction financing.
