# Situation Faction

[[cmf/Термины/Situation Faction|Situation Factions]] are [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страны]] that share a common interest in the outome of a [[cmf/Термины/International Situation|Situation]]. They are guided (directly or indirectly) by their [[cmf/Термины/Situation Leader|Situation Leaders]] and can either benefit or suffer from its eventual resolution.
