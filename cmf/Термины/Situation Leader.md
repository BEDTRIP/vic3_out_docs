# Situation Leader

One [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страна]] on each side of a [[cmf/Термины/Situation Faction|Faction]] is considered the [[cmf/Термины/Situation Leader|Situation Leader]] for that Faction. These are usually (but not always) the instigators of the [[cmf/Термины/International Situation|Situation]], and tend to be the drivers of progress through the concept_com_situation towards its eventual resolution.
