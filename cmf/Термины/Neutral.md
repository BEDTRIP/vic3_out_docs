# Neutral

[[cmf/Термины/Situation Faction|Neutral Situation Factions]] may be either a defined group with a third aim or simply a descriptive term for countries involved in or affected by the [[cmf/Термины/International Situation|Situation]]. Neutral Factions are [[vanilla/Термины/Страна|Страны]] loosely involved in a situation, but they may be swayed to join a side in the right circumstances.
