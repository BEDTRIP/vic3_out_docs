# Воздухоплавание

Ballooning is the earliest form of [[morgenrote/Термины/Авиация|Авиация]] in [[morgenrote/Термины/Morgenröte|Morgenröte]], and due to its inherent danger at the start of the game, it is reserved only for particularly daring adventurers. A [[morgenrote/Термины/Аэронавт (santos_balloonist)|Аэронавт]] can lead a peaceful life taking pleasure flights - or risk ambitious [[morgenrote/Термины/Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре|Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре]] flights and [[morgenrote/Термины/Перелёт на воздушном шаре|Перелёты на воздушном шаре]] that pose a mortal danger. On adventure expeditions, balloonists pursue their [[morgenrote/Термины/Учёный|Учёные]] interests and bring back useful items. As the game progresses, ballooning becomes increasingly professionalized and, with the Gordon Bennett Cup and flights into the stratosphere, evolves into a field that is highly regarded both in sports and science. Ballooning is intertwined with [[morgenrote/Термины/Метеорология|Метеорология]] in many ways. As well as the qualities of the balloonist, the characteristics of the [[morgenrote/Термины/Воздушный шар|Воздушный шар]] itself are also crucial to the success of a balloon flight.
