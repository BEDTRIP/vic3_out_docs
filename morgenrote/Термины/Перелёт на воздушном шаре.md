# Перелёт на воздушном шаре

Balloon crossings are the supreme challenge in [[morgenrote/Термины/Воздухоплавание|Воздухоплавание]], the one that brings the most recognition. At the same time it is the most dangerous undertaking, in which a [[morgenrote/Термины/Аэронавт|Аэронавт]] is most likely to lose their life. Seven of the nine balloon crossings are based on historical precedents; the crossings of the Strait of Korea and the Red Sea, on the other hand, are possible but have not been achieved within the Victoria 3 timeframe.
Balloon crossings require not only specific technologies but also that the [[morgenrote/Термины/Воздушный шар|Воздушный шар]] meets minimum requirements in terms of altitude and distance. However, it is advisable not to fly using only the minimum requirements; whilst this may be possible for simple crossings, it can lead to disaster even with moderate requirements. Experienced balloonists with good [[vanilla/Термины/Черта характера персонажа|Черты характера персонажа]], in particular, are best placed to successfully complete a balloon crossing. In particular, the 'ambitious' trait is an advantage, but the 'meteorologist' trait can also work wonders. If the balloonist is not a meteorologist themselves, the presence of a [[morgenrote/Термины/Метеоролог|Метеоролог]] has the same effect.
