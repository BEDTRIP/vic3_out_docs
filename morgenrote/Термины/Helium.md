# Helium

Helium is one of the rarest and hardest-to-obtain resources in [[morgenrote/Термины/Morgenröte|Morgenröte]]. Even the discovery of helium differs greatly from that of other [[morgenrote/Термины/Химический элемент|Химические элементы]]: Its theorization and discovery are linked to specific events in [[morgenrote/Термины/Астрономия|Астрономия]] (solar eclipses) and [[morgenrote/Термины/Геология|Геология]] (volcanic expeditions).
Only after helium has been isolated as an element can a [[morgenrote/Термины/Геолог|Геолог]] locate a helium deposit in a state while conducting geological research. As a prerequisite, the state must also have the technology Natural Gas Production. Once the Helium state trait has been added, a Helium Plant can be built in that state.

Helium is very rare: it occurs in only eight states in total—Texas, Alberta, Posen, Vrystaat, Sahara, Samara, Sichuan, and the Northern Territories. It is sufficient for a subject to own a helium plant in an owned state for the overlord to be considered a helium owner as well.

Helium plays a role in [[morgenrote/Термины/Авиация|Авиация]]. Balloons and zeppelins can be filled with helium. At first glance, helium doesn't actually have any special properties, since [[morgenrote/Термины/Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре|Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре]] flights using hydrogen are much more successful. However, helium does not have the disadvantage of being flammable—and is therefore considered significantly safer for [[morgenrote/Термины/Аэронавт|Аэронавты]] to avoid accidents, especially during [[morgenrote/Термины/Stratospheric Flight|Stratospheric Flights]]. There are rumors that the same applies to zeppelins as well...
