# Stratospheric Flight

Toward the end of the game, stratospheric flights become available as separate high-altitude flights for a [[morgenrote/Термины/Аэронавт|Аэронавт]]. This is considered a special form of [[morgenrote/Термины/Воздухоплавание|Воздухоплавание]] Here, altitude records beyond 12,000 meters can be achieved. Stratospheric flights are considered extremely dangerous and should only be undertaken with solid technical expertise and a skilled balloonist. A [[morgenrote/Термины/Метеоролог|Метеоролог]] is also required for the first launch of a stratospheric flight. Altitudes of up to 22,000 meters can be reached. Good luck!
