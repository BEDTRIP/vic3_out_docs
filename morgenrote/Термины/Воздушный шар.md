# Воздушный шар

The balloon is a [[morgenrote/Термины/Аэронавт|Аэронавт]]'s most important tool and plays a crucial role in [[morgenrote/Термины/Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре|Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре]] flights and [[morgenrote/Термины/Перелёт на воздушном шаре|Перелёты на воздушном шаре]]. Balloons can be adapted through technical advances; for example, through new technologies, specialisation in a particular type, or a change in fuel. Every balloon has specific values for altitude, distance and time. The average values are merely guidelines, as the characteristics of the balloonist also have an influence, and each value has a maximum and a minimum between which a balloon fluctuates during its flights. This means that the same balloon, with the same balloonist, may produce slightly different values during successive flights.
