# Рекорд полёта на воздушном шаре

Traditionally, a [[morgenrote/Термины/Аэронавт|Аэронавт]] attempts to set balloon records in order to attract international attention. The recognised categories for balloon records are altitude, distance and time. The prestige gained from this increases with the magnitude of the record: every 500 metres in altitude, every 100 kilometres in distance and every 3 hours in time are rewarded with one prestige point. The key factors in setting these records are, above all, the characteristics of the [[morgenrote/Термины/Воздушный шар|Воздушный шар]] itself, which can be improved through technology, specialisation and changes to the fuel. Added to this are the personal qualities of the balloonist and, in certain cases, the presence of a [[morgenrote/Термины/Математик|Математик]]. Record attempts in [[morgenrote/Термины/Воздухоплавание|Воздухоплавание]] are a risky undertaking and can, in some cases, result in the death of the balloonist.
