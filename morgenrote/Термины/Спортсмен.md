# Спортсмен

In [[morgenrote/Термины/Morgenröte|Morgenröte]] **Athletes** are special characters that allow you to use certain mechanics of the mod.**Athletes** are usually created through events or via a button. Like other characters, **Athletes** possess experiences that guarantee greater success in their field.
