# All Parties Banned

Censorship bans all parties, forcing them to be Institutionally-led 'factions' in the parliament. Outlawed Dissent bans all parties, and creates a mega-'faction' made out of Reactionaries and Institutional Interest Groups. Ideological Interest Groups are mostly independents.
