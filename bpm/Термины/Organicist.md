# Organicist

The **Christian Democrats**, the **Reactionaries** and the **Fascists** [[vanilla/Термины/Заинтересованная группа|Interest Groups]]. They believe that human society is like an organism, and individual humans are like cells of an organism -- seeking to empower those cells within the state, or have them become a part of the state.
